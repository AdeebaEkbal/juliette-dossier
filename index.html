SUBJECT: FERRARS, Juliette (True Identity: SOMMERS, Ella)

Vitals & Identification:
  • Status:High-Value Asset / Fugitive
  • Base of Operations:Omega Point(Formerly: Sector 45 Headquarters; Regional Asylum)
  • Physical Profile:5'3" with a thin, fragile build resulting from prolonged malnourishment. Long brown hair, blue-green eyes, and exceptionally pale skin due to 264 days of zero sunlight exposure in solitary confinement.

Background & Skillset:
Subject possesses unprecedented, god-tier superhuman abilities. Her primary anomaly is a lethal kinetic/energy-absorbing touch; prolonged skin-to-skin contact fatally drains the life force of any organic being. Subject has recently manifested secondary abilities, including extreme superhuman strength (capable of shattering concrete), near-indestructibility, and the ability to project kinetic shockwaves. She lacks formal combat training but her sheer raw power compensates for technical deficiencies.

Psychological Evaluation:
Subject suffers from profound psychological trauma stemming from lifelong parental abandonment and extreme isolation. She exhibits a severely fractured self-image, historically viewing herself as a monster and a "curse". To manage her internal chaos, she utilises obsessive counting and poetic journaling as grounding mechanisms. However, despite relentless abuse and efforts to weaponise her, she retains a stubborn, almost crippling degree of empathy. She actively refuses to inflict pain on the innocent and is highly resistant to standard authoritarian coercion.

Known Associates:
  • Aaron Warner:Chief Commander of Sector 45. Ability: Energy absorption (renders him immune to her touch). Initially her captor, he is highly fixated on the subject and actively encourages her to embrace her lethal potential.
  • Adam Kent:Former Reestablishment soldier. Ability: Power neutralisation. Acted as her initial tether to humanity and aided her escape from Sector 45.
  • Kenji Kishimoto:Operative for the Omega Point rebellion. Ability: Invisibility. Serves as her primary tactical ally and stabilising emotional anchor.

Operational Assessment:Subject possesses the raw destructive capability to single-handedly dismantle Reestablishment infrastructure. While initially paralysed by her own fear, she is rapidly gaining agency and control over her abilities. If she fully aligns with the Omega Point rebellion, she represents an apocalyptic-level threat to the Supreme Commander's regime.
